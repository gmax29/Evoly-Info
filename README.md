# Evoly

**Your own AI developer – living on your PC, not in someone else's cloud.**

Evoly is a personal AI assistant for Windows, created by **Gmax17/Gmax29**.
She runs entirely on your own computer: no cloud, no subscription, no data leaving your machine.
You talk to her in short, everyday sentences – and she gets to work.

---

## What Evoly is

Evoly is built to be more than a chatbot. She is a hands-on partner for building software:

- **She does the work, not just the talking.** Evoly reads and understands whole projects, plans the steps,
  writes and fixes code, builds it and starts the program.
- **She proves instead of promising.** After a change she actually uses the program – presses keys,
  clicks buttons, takes screenshots and looks at them – and checks the logic with her own test runs.
  Under every result she lists what was really verified, measured by the system itself.
- **A second pair of eyes.** Before she calls a job finished, an independent reviewer inside Evoly
  checks her work with a fresh mind and sends her back if something is wrong.
- **She doesn't give up halfway.** No arbitrary step limit: when an approach fails, she changes her
  strategy, gets a second opinion, and only asks you when a decision is really yours to make.
- **She listens when you say it's wrong.** If you report a problem twice, she treats it as proof that her
  last fix failed – reproduces it first, finds the real cause, then fixes it.
- **She sees, speaks and listens.** Evoly understands images and screenshots, can create images,
  understands your voice and answers with a natural voice of her own.
- **She remembers.** Projects, notes, decisions and your preferences stay with her across sessions,
  so you can pick up exactly where you left off.
- **She tunes herself to your hardware** to get the most speed out of your own graphics card.

## What Evoly is meant to become

A developer at your side who understands a single sentence like *"take a look at this project and
improve it"* – and then finds the real problems on her own, fixes them, proves the fix, and never
breaks what already worked.

And one step further: Evoly is designed to **improve herself**. She can work on her own source code
in a sealed-off copy. A change only counts when it builds cleanly and passes her complete self-test
suite of more than 800 checks – and even then it is only a proposal until her owner accepts it.
